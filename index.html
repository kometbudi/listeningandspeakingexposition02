Exposition Text: Social Media Issues

An Exposition Text is a text that gives arguments to persuade the listeners or readers about an issue. In Unit 2, we practice how to give good advice and arguments about everyday social media problems faced by high school students.

Generic Structure
  • 1. Thesis: States your opinion or stance about your friend's problem.
  • 2. Arguments: Gives clear reasons to support your opinion (reasons why it is good/bad).
  • 3. Recommendation: Gives practical advice on what your friend should or should not do.
Language Features
  • • Simple Present Tense: Stating facts (e.g., "Too much screen time makes us tired.").
  • • Modal Verbs (Advice): Should, ought to, must, need to (e.g., "You should turn off your phone at night.").
  • • Connectives: First, In addition, Therefore, Consequently.
Useful Expressions

Giving Advice / Memberi Saran:

"I recommend that you set a daily screen limit."

Expressing Opinion / Pendapat:

"In my opinion, we must protect our privacy online."

Adding Arguments / Alasan Tambahan:

"In addition, spending time offline makes us happier."
